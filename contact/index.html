Contact BAITSSS

Start with the problem you are trying to solve.

A first conversation can be about a field, study area, research question, irrigation problem, water-management need, or technical workflow. You do not need to decide in advance whether the answer is a BAITSSS analysis service, research collaboration, software use, or integration project.

Before contacting us

Useful starting information is simple: where the project is, the period of interest, what data or observations already exist, and what decision or scientific question needs an answer.

Useful first message

Four things help us understand the project quickly.

A short description is enough. Detailed technical specifications can come later if the problem is a reasonable fit.

1. PlaceField, farm, district, basin, research site, or study area.
2. PeriodSingle event, growing season, year, multi-year study, or another time window.
3. QuestionWhat needs to be understood, compared, estimated, investigated, or supported.
4. EvidenceAvailable irrigation, weather, soil, field observations, remote sensing, or other project data.
