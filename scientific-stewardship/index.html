Scientific Stewardship

Scientific interpretation

BAITSSS outputs depend on input data, model configuration, field conditions, and model version.

Output classes

Observed data, supplied inputs, and modeled quantities are identified separately.

01 · Data and model outputs

Observed, supplied, and modeled quantities

Observed

Observations

Satellite measurements, meteorological observations or forcing products, field measurements, and independent records retain their native spatial and temporal scales.

Supplied

Model inputs

Project geometry, dates, soil properties, rooting assumptions, management information, and configured parameters define the simulation setup.

Modeled

Model outputs

ET components, soil-water states, modeled irrigation, resistance behavior, and simulated temperatures are calculated quantities.

02 · Interpretation

Key distinctions

Irrigation

Modeled irrigation

Modeled irrigation is not documented farmer-applied irrigation unless independent irrigation records establish that correspondence.

Comparison

Model–observation differences

Differences may reflect forcing data, parameters, model structure, spatial scale, timing, or measurement uncertainty.

03 · Temporal simulation

Continuous hourly accounting

Irrigation, rainfall, transpiration, soil evaporation, drainage, and root-zone depletion continue between satellite observations. BAITSSS represents these processes with an hourly water and energy balance.
