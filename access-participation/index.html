How would you like to engage with BAITSSS?

Access is organized around scientific purpose, the stage of software development, and the responsibilities needed for responsible interpretation and collaboration.

Responsibilities are agreed together

BAITSSS

  • Describe the software and scientific status accurately.
  • Provide the agreed materials and version context.
  • Keep known assumptions and application boundaries visible.

Research user / institution

  • Use appropriate input data and document provenance.
  • Interpret modeled outputs within the intended application.
  • Distinguish modeled irrigation from documented applied irrigation.

Prepare your request

Tell us who you are, the scientific or professional purpose, the site or dataset if known, and what kind of participation you are requesting. Access arrangements depend on the development stage and proposed use.
