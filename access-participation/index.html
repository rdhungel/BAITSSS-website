Access & Participation

Ways to work with BAITSSS Desktop and the BAITSSS model.

Research groups, institutions, agencies, and projects can identify the collaboration pathway that matches their scientific or professional purpose. Software access rules are maintained separately in Access Principles.

01 · Access pathways

Ways to use BAITSSS Desktop and collaborate around the BAITSSS model

01

Academic research / teaching

Universities, laboratories, faculty, and students may use BAITSSS Desktop for research, graduate projects, teaching, and scientific investigation through an appropriate institutional arrangement.

02

Scientific validation

Projects centered on independent evaluation can use the collaboration route to discuss scope, data responsibilities, and an appropriate evaluation plan.

03

Government / agency

Public agencies and research organizations may collaborate on BAITSSS model applications in agricultural water, evapotranspiration, irrigation, environmental, or programmatic research through a defined project or organizational arrangement.

04

Commercial / professional

Professional and commercial applications may be considered where BAITSSS Desktop and the BAITSSS model are scientifically appropriate. Scope, access, responsibilities, and support are defined for the intended use.

05

Scientific collaboration

Researchers and organizations with an existing project, dataset, study site, or proposal may collaborate on BAITSSS model application, evaluation, comparative studies, scientific development, and related research. Project-supported development or training can also be discussed where appropriate.

06

Demonstration / review

Researchers, institutions, reviewers, and prospective collaborators can first examine the BAITSSS model through the scientific evidence and publications, and BAITSSS Desktop through the website, documentation, videos, and demonstrations, before discussing software access or collaboration.

02 · Documented collaborations

The BAITSSS model has developed through collaboration across universities, agencies, water managers, and student research.

These examples are part of the documented scientific record. They are presented together so no single project is mistaken for the full collaboration history.

USDA-NIFA · UC RIVERSIDE

AIM-AI multi-institutional research

The BAITSSS model has been used within the USDA-NIFA funded Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Water, Nutrient, Salinity, and Pest Management in the Western U.S. project led by UC Riverside, including specialty-crop water and irrigation research and collaboration across participating institutions.

USDA-NIFA project record ↗

KANSAS GMD4 · KANSAS STATE · USDA-ARS

Groundwater and irrigation management

The BAITSSS model was applied with Kansas Groundwater Management District No. 4, Kansas State University, and USDA-ARS collaborators to examine landscape-scale evapotranspiration, modeled irrigation, reported irrigation, and restricted water allocations in northwest Kansas.

USDA-ARS publication record ↗

KANSAS STATE · USDA-ARS

Field evaluation and crop-water research

Collaborative work at Bushland, Texas brought Kansas State University and USDA-ARS scientists together to evaluate the BAITSSS model against field observations and to investigate crop water use, energy balance behavior, and irrigation-management applications.

USDA-ARS evaluation record ↗

USDA-ARS · UC RIVERSIDE

Specialty-crop evapotranspiration research

Later BAITSSS model research in California included USDA-ARS and UC Riverside collaboration on citrus evapotranspiration, independent eddy-covariance comparison, OpenET comparison, and specialty-crop water-use questions.

USDA-ARS citrus publication record ↗

MIT STUDENT RESEARCH

Higher-resolution data for irrigation management

A documented MIT student collaboration used the BAITSSS model within a university research project in Cheyenne County, Kansas to investigate higher-resolution satellite information for evapotranspiration and irrigation analysis.

03 · Project collaboration

Planning a project involving BAITSSS Desktop or the BAITSSS model?

We welcome discussions with universities, research organizations, agencies, and other partners developing proposals or already leading funded projects where BAITSSS Desktop or the BAITSSS model may be useful. Contact us early when project-specific analysis, validation, development, training, or other substantial participation may need to be included in the project scope.
