2019 · Independent evaluation

Uncalibrated ET evaluation in a semiarid, advective climate

Bushland, Texas · Hydrological Processes

Question: How does BAITSSS perform without prior site calibration when compared with independent ET and surface measurements under strongly advective conditions?

The study used a 127-day corn growing season and compared simulated ET with weighing-lysimeter observations.

2024 · Multi-source intercomparison

Citrus evapotranspiration intercomparison

San Joaquin Valley, California · Agricultural Water Management · Open access

Question: How do BAITSSS and OpenET ET estimates compare with eddy covariance observations and water received in citrus orchards?

The study evaluated navel and mandarin orchards over 2021–2022.

Two-year mean annual cumulative ET
EC 908 mm · OpenET ensemble 1169 mm · BAITSSS 867 mm · water received 886 mm. The paper reports that BAITSSS totals were within about ±10% of water received for all site-year combinations; performance against EC varied by orchard and year.

Spatial irrigation pattern

The same published synthesis shows a center-pivot example in SD-6 LEMA where seasonal vegetation patterns corresponded with three broad categories of simulated cumulative irrigation.

Published spatial example · 2013

Three irrigation sectors visible within one center pivot

80–160 mmlower cumulative irrigation sector
240–320 mmintermediate cumulative irrigation sector
400–480 mmhigher cumulative irrigation sector

The published discussion relates these spatial differences primarily to seasonal and pixel-scale vegetation variation, with additional influence from soil water-holding characteristics. The authors also note that these simulated spatial patterns could not be fully verified against actual within-pivot irrigation conditions.

This website summary preserves that limitation rather than presenting the simulated sectors as measured irrigation.
