Research & project inquiries

Bring the problem first.

Students, advisors, researchers, agencies, consultants, and organizations can begin with the question they are trying to solve. You do not need to decide first whether the right path is software, project analysis, training, or collaboration.

Send four things.

A short first message is enough. These four items let us make an initial fit assessment without turning the first conversation into a software-access request.

01 · PLACE

Where?

Field, farm, district, basin, research site, or study area.

02 · PERIOD

When?

Event, growing season, year, multi-year period, or another time window.

03 · QUESTION

What needs an answer?

What do you need to understand, compare, estimate, investigate, or support?

04 · EVIDENCE

What already exists?

Available irrigation, weather, soil, field observations, remote sensing, or other data.

What happens next

First check the fit. Then choose the pathway.

If BAITSSS is a reasonable scientific and technical fit, the next step can be sized to the project: short guidance, a demonstration, project-specific analysis, research collaboration, training, BAITSSS Desktop use, or an organizational discussion. Substantial work is scoped before it begins.

Student or source-code request?

Student projects are welcome. When a request involves substantial technical support, software access, or source code, the advisor or supervisor should join the discussion so the research objective, responsibilities, and appropriate pathway can be defined together. Source-code transfer is not automatic.
