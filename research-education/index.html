Research & Education

Research and educational applications

BAITSSS provides a desktop implementation of the model for research, teaching, sensitivity analysis, and independent evaluation.

Scope

Applications include model structure, parameter sensitivity, environmental forcing, comparison data, and reproducible simulation workflows.

01 · Research applications

Research topics

01

Resistance architecture

Evaluation of aerodynamic, canopy, soil, stability, and connectivity assumptions.

02

Thermal observations

Comparison of surface-temperature observations with modeled temperature states.

03

Soil and vegetation sensitivity

Parameter sensitivity, rooting assumptions, vegetation representation, and flux partitioning.

04

Crop and site transferability

Model behavior across environments, crops, management conditions, and meteorological forcing.

05

Model comparison

Comparison with independent ET products, field observations, and other modeling approaches.

06

Spatial performance

Evaluation from representative points to gridded applications and heterogeneous landscapes.

02 · Education

Model processes and reproducible analysis

PHYSICS

Evapotranspiration processes

Radiation, resistance, soil evaporation, transpiration, and atmospheric demand.

STATE

Water and energy balance

Water storage and surface-energy fluxes through time.

EXPERIMENT

Sensitivity experiments

Controlled changes in assumptions and their effects on modeled states and fluxes.

REPRODUCIBILITY

Reproducible workflows

Project inputs, run records, checkpoints, and outputs for repeated analysis.

03 · Independent evaluation

Model evaluation

Independent evaluation can reproduce a defined model setup, document the assumptions being tested, and compare model results with appropriate observations or other estimates.
