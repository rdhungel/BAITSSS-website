Research & Education

Use the software to investigate the science.

BAITSSS reduces repetitive computational setup while keeping assumptions, sensitivity, evidence, and interpretation visible.

Research directions

Questions BAITSSS can help investigate

Resistance architecture

Evaluate aerodynamic, canopy, soil, stability, and connectivity assumptions.

Thermal observations

Investigate how surface-temperature observations can help evaluate modeled states.

Soil & vegetation sensitivity

Test parameter sensitivity, rooting assumptions, vegetation representation, and flux partitioning.

Crop & site transferability

Examine behavior across environments, crops, management conditions, and meteorological forcing.

Model comparison

Compare BAITSSS with independent ET products, field observations, and other modeling approaches.

Spatial performance

Study behavior from representative points to gridded applications and heterogeneous landscapes.

Sensitivity experiments

Reproducible workflows

Use project inputs, run records, checkpoints, and outputs to reproduce and review scientific work.

Independent evaluation

Compare the model with appropriate evidence

Independent evaluation is an important part of BAITSSS research. The desktop workflow makes it easier to reproduce a setup, identify the assumptions being tested, compare results with suitable observations, and determine whether a difference comes from the software or the science.

When results disagree: investigate the observation, inputs, assumptions, scale, timing, and model behavior before treating the difference as a software defect.
