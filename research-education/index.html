Research & Education

Keep the scientific questions at the center.

BAITSSS Desktop can support coursework, student projects, graduate research, extension education, and collaborative studies where a reproducible field-scale modeling environment serves the academic question.

What BAITSSS changes

Students and researchers can begin from a working field-scale environment instead of spending the first part of a project rebuilding data preparation, execution, recovery, Results, and provenance infrastructure.

01 · Where it fits

One environment across teaching, research, and extension.

Different users can approach the same field from different questions while keeping the data, simulation, Results, and scientific record together.

CLASSROOM

Teach with a real field-scale system

Use ET, irrigation, hydrology, remote sensing, crop science, soil science, and agricultural engineering questions inside one working environment.

GRADUATE RESEARCH

Start with the research question

Investigate assumptions, sensitivity, spatial variability, irrigation scenarios, observations, and model limitations without first constructing the full application framework.

FACULTY

Use one reproducible environment for supervision

Support demonstrations, assignments, theses, dissertations, capstones, model evaluation, and field studies with traceable project and run settings.

EXTENSION

Connect university science with field interpretation

Use the same scientific framework for field demonstrations, irrigation education, and interpretation of ET, soil-water, and satellite information.

WATER & ATMOSPHERIC SCIENCE

Study forcing, exchange, and field response

Examine how hourly weather, aerodynamic exchange, surface energy balance, vegetation, and soil-water state interact through time.

INTERDISCIPLINARY WORK

Let several disciplines inspect the same simulation

Agronomy, engineering, soil science, remote sensing, hydrology, crop science, atmospheric science, and extension can work from the same field record.

02 · Research frontier

Software can remove infrastructure work. It cannot remove the scientific problem.

Seasonal hourly simulation, 30 m spatial analysis, multi-source environmental inputs, restartable execution, repeatable scenarios, and traceable Results are easier to organize when the software framework already exists. The difficult work remains observation, interpretation, transferability, and validation.

NOW MORE FEASIBLE

Controlled field-scale computational experiments

Comparable seasonal and spatial experiments can be organized inside one reusable workflow instead of being rebuilt separately for each study.

STILL DIFFICULT

Observation and uncertainty

Irrigation records, root-zone measurements, thermal observations, ET partitioning, parameter transferability, and long multi-site validation remain difficult scientific problems.

THE BAITSSS BRIDGE

Move effort toward the question

The project, data, execution, recovery, Results, provenance, and parameter framework is already present, leaving more time for mechanism, sensitivity, observations, and evaluation.

A decade ago versus now

Many underlying datasets and models existed a decade ago, but students often had to assemble downloading, preprocessing, reprojection, timestamp handling, runtime dependencies, long-run recovery, plotting, and provenance as separate research code. BAITSSS Desktop turns more of that work into reusable infrastructure.

The remaining frontier is not solved by software alone.

BAITSSS can make experiments easier to define, repeat, compare, and inspect, but it cannot replace lysimeters, eddy-covariance systems, soil-water measurements, thermal observations, irrigation records, or careful field experimental design.

03 · Academic package
In preparation

A direct path into the science

The academic package is being organized so students and instructors can begin with a defined problem and a working computational environment. It is not yet presented as a finished packaged course product.

  • BAITSSS Desktop
  • Example project and data
  • Short getting-started tutorial
  • One complete classroom exercise
  • Instructor guidance
  • Explanation of the science behind each workflow
  • CSV and figure outputs for analysis
  • Suggested undergraduate, MS, and PhD project directions
Programming and scientific computing remain important.

BAITSSS Desktop is not intended to replace programming or scientific-computing education. It provides the computational framework when the primary objective is to study evapotranspiration, soil water, irrigation, remote sensing, model behavior, and scientific interpretation.

04 · Research directions

Questions students and researchers can investigate

01

Resistance and stability

Evaluate aerodynamic, canopy, soil, and stability assumptions, including conditions that affect hourly numerical behavior.

02

Thermal observations

Compare independent surface-temperature observations with modeled temperature states.

03

Soil and vegetation sensitivity

Test soil properties, rooting assumptions, vegetation representation, and flux partitioning.

04

Crop and site transferability

Examine behavior across crops, climates, management settings, and meteorological conditions.

05

Model comparison

Design research exercises around comparison, interpretation, sensitivity, and reproducible analysis.

06

Spatial performance

Examine behavior from representative points to gridded fields and heterogeneous landscapes.

05 · Academic participation

Research, teaching, and extension through institutions

Academic and extension use is organized through the responsible university, department, laboratory, extension program, or research organization so the software, study context, and scientific responsibilities are clear.
