Research & Education

Use the software to investigate the science.

BAITSSS is being developed to reduce repetitive computational setup while keeping scientific assumptions, sensitivity, evidence, and interpretation visible.

Research directions

Questions the platform can help investigate

Resistance architecture

Evaluate aerodynamic, canopy, soil, stability, and connectivity assumptions.

Thermal observations

Investigate how surface-temperature observations can constrain or diagnose modeled states.

Soil & vegetation sensitivity

Test parameter sensitivity, rooting assumptions, vegetation representation, and flux partitioning.

Crop & site transferability

Examine behavior across environments, crops, management conditions, and meteorological forcing.

Model comparison

Compare BAITSSS with independent ET products, field observations, and other modeling approaches.

Spatial performance

Study behavior from representative points through gridded applications and heterogeneous landscapes.

Education

Teach the physical links rather than hiding them

Physical evapotranspiration

Connect radiation, resistance, soil evaporation, transpiration, and atmospheric demand.

Water and energy balance

Follow the coupled accounting of water storage and surface-energy fluxes through time.

Sensitivity experiments

Change controlled assumptions and inspect their effect on later modeled states and fluxes.

Reproducible workflows

Use explicit project inputs, execution records, checkpoints, and outputs to support transparent scientific work.

Independent evaluation

Challenge the model with appropriate evidence

Independent evaluation is part of the intended research pathway. The desktop workflow should make it easier to reproduce a setup, identify the assumptions being tested, compare results with suitable observations, and separate software behavior from scientific disagreement.

Interpretation principle: disagreement with an observation or comparison product should be investigated scientifically before it is treated as a software defect.
