Capabilities

What BAITSSS can do as one integrated scientific system.

BAITSSS connects field definition, environmental data, hourly water and energy balance, evapotranspiration, soil water, irrigation analysis, thermal computation, observed Landsat thermal comparison, mapped Results, pixel scale extraction, and scientific export within one field scale modeling workflow.

Integrated scale

More than an ET map and more than a soil water model.

BAITSSS brings the major stages of field scale analysis together so a user can examine how evapotranspiration, soil water, irrigation, vegetation condition, thermal behavior, and spatial variability evolve through the same simulation.

05

Continuous hourly state progression

BAITSSS advances the simulation hour by hour while carrying the surface and root zone state from each completed hour into the next. This supports both short diagnostic runs and long seasonal simulations.

10

Satellite thermal is not required as a direct model input

The current BAITSSS route does not assimilate satellite thermal imagery directly. Thermal states are computed internally within the model. At supported Landsat acquisition times, observed surface temperature can also be extracted at the selected model pixel for independent comparison with the modeled thermal behavior.

Results and extraction

Move from the field wide picture to pixel scale behavior.

15

Mapped Results

  • Spatial model output inspection
  • Field scale variability
  • Run specific Results
  • Total ET map export
16

Pixel scale extraction

  • Select an exact 30 m model pixel
  • Extract hourly ET, soil water, irrigation, and thermal model behavior
  • Carry the selected pixel through the full simulation period
  • Include observed Landsat thermal values at their exact acquisition times when available
17

Scientific outputs

  • Clean hourly and selected pixel CSV results
  • General and publication PNG figures and maps
  • Simulation Report PDF and run information
  • Provenance and diagnostics retained separately from analysis tables
18

Modeled and observed thermal inspection

Internally calculated thermal states can be examined together with Landsat observed surface temperature at acquisition times. The observed Landsat values are retained as independent comparison data rather than assimilated into the model calculation.

Capability status

Established capability and future extension are kept separate.

Established BAITSSS capability

  • 30 m Landsat based field modeling
  • Hourly NLDAS driven simulation
  • Two source energy balance
  • Two layer soil water balance
  • Internal soil and canopy temperature computation
  • ET, soil water, and modeled irrigation analysis
  • Mapped Results and full period pixel scale extraction
  • Landsat thermal observation comparison at acquisition time
  • Clean scientific CSV, PNG, PDF, diagnostics, and provenance outputs
  • Long runs, checkpoints, resume, and recovery

Future extension

  • Additional satellite platforms beyond the currently supported Landsat route
  • Alternative weather data routes
  • Other capabilities not yet incorporated into the maintained BAITSSS route
Capability continuity: the scientific capability set presented here is established through the BAITSSS research and V7 system. The V8 desktop generation carries that proven scientific foundation into the permanent product architecture and integrated desktop workflow.
