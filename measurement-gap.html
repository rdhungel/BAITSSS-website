The measurement gap

Applied irrigation fell sharply. Did crop water demand fall with it?

BAITSSS provides an independent estimate of expected crop water use and irrigation requirement from vegetation, weather, soil, and water-balance conditions. That reference helps distinguish a change in irrigation management from a change in environmental demand.

What BAITSSS adds

Applied irrigation changed far more than the modeled crop water requirement. The decade-long comparison shows that lower water use during LEMA was not accompanied by a comparable decline in BAITSSS-estimated irrigation requirement or evapotranspiration.
