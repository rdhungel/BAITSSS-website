What are you buying with BAITSSS?

BAITSSS Desktop V1 is a complete field-scale water monitoring system that runs on your own computer. You are buying the working capability itself: define your field, prepare the required data, run your own simulations, examine the results, compare runs, and retain your own field record.

The purpose is simple: put useful field-water capability directly in the hands of farmers, researchers, consultants, educators, and the water-management community. The detailed technical inventory remains on the Capabilities page.

Free ET products

Why use BAITSSS when ET maps are already available?

If a user only needs an ET estimate or map, an existing free ET product may already meet that need. BAITSSS does not need to replace those products. It serves a different purpose when the user wants to perform and retain a fuller field investigation locally.

ET products

A valuable view of consumptive water use

Satellite-based ET products can provide important estimates of how much water was consumed over a field and are useful for monitoring, accounting, research, and water-management applications.

BAITSSS Desktop V1

Investigate the field around the ET result

BAITSSS couples total ET with separate vegetation and soil ET, surface and root-zone soil-water states, irrigation behavior, vegetation, hourly state evolution, spatial Results, selected-pixel history, and supported scenario reruns inside a persistent project.

Where BAITSSS stands: use a free ET product when that is enough. Use BAITSSS when you want the working system on your own computer and need to investigate the field in greater depth.
