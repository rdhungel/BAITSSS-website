Business

Commercial pathways for BAITSSS technology.

BAITSSS welcomes commercial licensing, technology integration, strategic partnership, sponsored development, and technology acquisition discussions. Organizations in agriculture, irrigation, water technology, geospatial systems, remote sensing, and related fields are welcome to discuss fit and next steps.

Business pathways

Ways to work with BAITSSS.

Each discussion starts from the existing BAITSSS Desktop V1 product and scientific engine, then defines the commercial or technical path that is relevant to the organization.

Commercial licensing

Professional use under defined terms

Discuss licensing arrangements for use of BAITSSS Desktop or related BAITSSS technology in a professional or commercial setting.

Technology integration

Connect BAITSSS with an existing system

Discuss integration with organization-specific data, sensors, APIs, storage, cloud services, or operational workflows.

Strategic partnership

Develop a defined joint pathway

Discuss a strategic collaboration where BAITSSS technology contributes to a broader agriculture, irrigation, geospatial, or water-technology product or service.

Sponsored / custom development

Extend the platform for a defined need

Discuss project-supported development where an organization needs specific data connections, workflow extensions, interfaces, or deployment capabilities.

Technology acquisition

Discuss broader transfer or acquisition interest

Organizations may also initiate a confidential discussion regarding broader strategic interest in the BAITSSS software, scientific engine, architecture, and associated technology.
