Projects & services

Bring the water problem. We can work on the analysis with you.

BAITSSS is not only a desktop product to install and learn. For a defined field, study area, research question, or water-management need, the BAITSSS team can help frame the problem, perform project-specific analysis, and deliver interpretable maps, time series, model outputs, and technical results.

Start with the question

You do not have to become the software operator first.

A useful project begins with the decision, research question, field, district, or technical problem. We can determine whether BAITSSS is appropriate, what data are needed, what can be analyzed defensibly, and what form of result is useful.

Field & water-use analysis

Understand ET and field-water behavior through time

Evaluate evapotranspiration, vegetation and soil contributions, root-zone and surface soil-water states, irrigation behavior, spatial patterns, selected locations, and seasonal change for a defined project.

Irrigation & management questions

Investigate how a field responds to water and management

Use the model and project record to examine irrigation timing, drying and refill behavior, seasonal water demand, alternative supported scenarios, and differences among locations or periods.

Research & technical studies

Use BAITSSS as the analysis environment, not as the research question

Support university, agency, consulting, and collaborative studies that need a reproducible satellite-to-field workflow without rebuilding the complete scientific software stack for every project.

Examples of problems

Project work can begin from a practical need.

The examples below are deliberately problem-led. They are not claims that one method answers every water question, and they are not a substitute for defining the project scope and available evidence.

How did field-scale water use change through a season?Develop maps and time series of ET and associated field-water states for the study period.
Where are water-use or soil-water patterns different within a field or study area?Inspect spatial outputs and selected locations to identify patterns worth further investigation.
How did irrigation and drying behavior evolve between events?Use the hourly model state and project history to examine field response over time.
Can an existing research or management project use BAITSSS without building a new modeling pipeline?Define the analysis together, prepare the required data, run the system, and deliver the technical outputs needed for the project.
If a freely available ET product already answers the question, that may be the right tool. BAITSSS becomes useful when the project needs a deeper field investigation, hourly process information, soil-water and irrigation context, reproducible reruns, or a project-specific analysis workflow.
Ways to work together

The engagement can match the problem.

Not every organization needs the same pathway. A project may be a one-time technical analysis, a research collaboration, use of BAITSSS Desktop by the organization, or a larger integration and development effort.

Analysis service

Project-specific analysis and technical results

Define the study area, period, available information, and question. We perform the agreed analysis and provide the resulting maps, time series, exports, interpretation, and technical record appropriate to the scope.

Research collaboration

Work together around a scientific question

Use BAITSSS within a funded or collaborative research project where responsibilities, data, scientific interpretation, outputs, and publication expectations are defined in advance.

Software & integration

Use or extend the system inside an organization

For organizations that need the capability directly, discuss Desktop access, licensing, integration, sponsored development, or a defined technical partnership.
