What are you buying with BAITSSS?

BAITSSS Desktop V1 is a complete field-scale water monitoring system that runs on your own computer. You are buying the working capability itself: define your field, prepare the required data, run your own simulations, examine the results, compare runs, and retain your own field record.

The purpose is simple: put useful field-water capability directly in the hands of farmers, researchers, consultants, educators, and the water-management community. The detailed technical inventory remains on the Capabilities page.

Why BAITSSS

A product built around ownership, independence, and practical use.

BAITSSS is not asking every user to build a sensor network, learn scientific programming, or depend entirely on another commercial platform. The product packages the difficult scientific and computational work into a desktop system the user can operate directly.

Freedom & ownershipEase & understandingKnowledge & transfer
1 · Own the capability

Run the water-monitoring system yourself

BAITSSS Desktop V1 runs on your own computer. Your projects, runs, maps, time series, comparisons, and exports remain available to you rather than existing only inside somebody else's online service.

Why it matters: use the results for your own understanding and as technical context when discussing field water use with consultants, districts, researchers, or agencies.
2 · No dense sensor network required

Start field-scale analysis without building an extensive field system

Many monitoring systems depend on combinations of sensors, telemetry, controllers, gateways, installation, maintenance, and recurring data services. BAITSSS can perform its field-scale analysis from the environmental and remote-sensing inputs used by the Desktop V1 workflow.

Why it matters: sensors can still add valuable independent observations, but an extensive installed sensor network is not the foundation required to begin using BAITSSS.
3 · Reduce platform dependence

Start from accessible environmental data and run locally

BAITSSS uses maintained Landsat, NLDAS, soil, land-cover, elevation, and project information to prepare and run the analysis. The core workflow does not require the user to purchase access to another commercial monitoring platform.

Why it matters: obtain the required data, prepare the project in BAITSSS, run the simulation on your computer, and keep the resulting analysis under your control.
4 · Use the system instead of building it

You should not have to become the BAITSSS developer

Field-scale scientific analysis normally requires remote sensing, raster preparation, weather processing, scientific libraries, model configuration, execution control, and Results handling. BAITSSS puts those pieces into one desktop workflow.

Why it matters: the user works with the field and the Results instead of assembling the scientific and geospatial software stack from the beginning.
5 · Build a continuing field record

Your field knowledge does not have to reset every season

Farm knowledge grows over years and often across generations. Repeated BAITSSS projects and simulations can preserve maps, time series, water conditions, irrigation behavior, and seasonal comparisons as a technical record of the same land.

Why it matters: accumulated experience can be compared with accumulated scientific records instead of relying only on memory from a single season.
6 · Portable field-scale computation

Carry the monitoring system with the laptop

BAITSSS runs locally. Once the project data are prepared, simulations can run on the user's computer while other work continues, and Results can be reviewed on the same machine afterward.

Why it matters: the analytical capability can travel with the user rather than requiring a dedicated remote platform. Simulation time depends on field size, period, computer, and run configuration.
7 · Self-contained desktop product

No separate Python installation or scientific software stack

BAITSSS Desktop V1 packages its scientific runtime with the application. The user does not need to install Python, construct environments, or manually assemble the model's scientific libraries before running the product.

Why it matters: the research implementation becomes usable software rather than a collection of code that first has to be configured by a specialist.
8 · Investigate without building the science

Learn your field by using the system repeatedly

The user can examine ET, soil-water conditions, irrigation, vegetation, maps, time series, selected locations, and supported management scenarios without writing the equations or programming the model.

Why it matters: use the science without having to build the science yourself, and develop practical understanding from repeated investigation of your own field.
9 · Teach with a real field

Show the next generation how crops and water change through a season

A producer, parent, teacher, or advisor can use a real field to explore questions such as how much water the crop used, when the field became drier, when irrigation occurred, and how conditions changed through the season.

Why it matters: the field itself can become a learning environment, connecting practical farm experience with visible scientific results.
10 · Make field water easier to understand

The science can remain complex. Using the information should not be.

Weather, vegetation, soil, energy balance, irrigation, and time interact in complicated ways. BAITSSS performs that scientific work underneath and organizes the resulting field information into a usable desktop workflow.

Why it matters: the product exists to make a difficult water problem easier to investigate, compare, and communicate.
11 · Technology transfer

The product is the vehicle. The capability is what transfers.

BAITSSS moves scientific methods, data preparation, model execution, field-scale analysis, reproducibility, and accumulated technical knowledge from a specialist research environment into a packaged product that other people can operate.

Why it matters: this is more than access to a finished map. It is direct access to a working scientific capability.
Free ET products

Why use BAITSSS when ET maps are already available?

If a user only needs an ET estimate or map, an existing free ET product may already meet that need. BAITSSS does not need to replace those products. It serves a different purpose when the user wants to perform and retain a fuller field investigation locally.

ET products

A valuable view of consumptive water use

Satellite-based ET products can provide important estimates of how much water was consumed over a field and are useful for monitoring, accounting, research, and water-management applications.

BAITSSS Desktop V1

Investigate the field around the ET result

BAITSSS couples total ET with separate vegetation and soil ET, surface and root-zone soil-water states, irrigation behavior, vegetation, hourly state evolution, spatial Results, selected-pixel history, and supported scenario reruns inside a persistent project.

Where BAITSSS stands: use a free ET product when that is enough. Use BAITSSS when you want the working system on your own computer and need to investigate the field in greater depth.
