Research Geography & Timeline

Where the research happened, and how it developed.

BAITSSS was not developed from a single field or one isolated experiment. The scientific record spans multiple agricultural settings, comparison datasets, water-management questions, and later software development.

The map below is a schematic research-geography view for orientation. It does not show exact field coordinates, and each publication remains the authority for its study site and methods.

Research through time

The scientific record evolved from process representation between satellite observations to field evaluation, water-management applications, multi-source comparison, and current desktop software.

2016

Between overpasses

Formalized the approach for carrying water and energy states through the interval between satellite observations.

2019

Independent ET evaluation

Tested BAITSSS against independent field measurements in a semiarid, strongly advective environment.

2020

Water-allocation application

Extended the model into landscape-scale irrigation and restricted-water analysis in northwest Kansas.

2024

Multi-source intercomparison

Compared citrus ET estimates with eddy covariance, OpenET ensemble output, and water received.

2026

Desktop software

Current development reorganizes the research model into a Windows desktop scientific workflow with project, data, execution, Results, export, provenance, and restart capability.

The continuity is scientific, not merely visual. The desktop software is the current implementation layer built on the earlier research record; the publication history and the software-verification record remain separate evidence sources.
