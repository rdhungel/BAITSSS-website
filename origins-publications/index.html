Origins & Publications

Scientific development over time.

BAITSSS developed through research on evapotranspiration, surface energy balance, soil-water accounting, irrigation, remote sensing, and later desktop-software modernization.

Development timeline

01

Scientific foundation

Remote-sensing and surface-energy-balance research established the physical modeling foundation.

02

BAITSSS formulation

The model evolved toward a coupled two-source energy-balance and soil-water framework.

03

Research applications

Field and regional studies extended evaluation across agricultural and water-management questions.

04

Computational modernization

The scientific codebase was reworked for faster, more traceable, restartable execution.

05

Desktop productization

The current effort is translating the research model into a structured desktop scientific application.
