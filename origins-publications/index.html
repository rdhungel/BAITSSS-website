01 · Scientific origin

Between satellite observations

Field processes continue between satellite observations. The BAITSSS model was developed to carry the water and energy balance through those intervals using remote sensing, weather, vegetation information, soil properties, and management assumptions.

2019 · Independent evaluation

Uncalibrated ET evaluation in a semiarid, advective climate

Bushland, Texas · Hydrological Processes

Question: How does the BAITSSS model perform without prior site calibration when compared with independent ET and surface measurements under strongly advective conditions?

The study used a 127-day corn growing season and compared simulated ET with weighing-lysimeter observations.

2024 · Multi-source intercomparison

Citrus evapotranspiration intercomparison

San Joaquin Valley, California · Agricultural Water Management · Open access

Question: How do BAITSSS model and OpenET ET estimates compare with eddy covariance observations and water received in citrus orchards?

The study evaluated navel and mandarin orchards over 2021–2022. BAITSSS model ET results were generated using actual irrigation records rather than model-simulated irrigation.

Two-year mean annual cumulative ET
EC 908 mm · OpenET ensemble 1169 mm · BAITSSS model 867 mm · water received 886 mm. The paper reports that BAITSSS model totals were within about ±10% of water received for all site-year combinations; performance against EC varied by orchard and year.

03 · Software verification

Tests of the BAITSSS Desktop execution system

These tests address whether BAITSSS Desktop executes the intended BAITSSS model scientific workflow reliably. They are not substitutes for field validation.

01

Numerical equivalence

BAITSSS Desktop execution methods are compared with the accepted BAITSSS model scientific reference under controlled conditions and defined numerical criteria.

02

Checkpoint and restart

Stop, resume, restart, and run-lifecycle behavior are tested separately so continuity of scientific state is not assumed from interface behavior.

03

Run identity and provenance

BAITSSS Desktop project inputs, run configuration, software state, outputs, and execution records are preserved so a result can be traced back to the run that produced it.

04 · Computational evidence

Performance is reported as a workload measurement

Benchmarking rule

Workload, hardware, and timing belong together

Runtime is meaningful only with the size of the simulated area, number of model hours, execution configuration, and computer used for the test.

Current performance observations →
Interpretation boundary

Speed is not scientific accuracy

A faster execution route is useful only if the scientific state and outputs remain consistent with the accepted reference under the defined verification criteria.

05 · Ongoing scientific comparison

Independent observations and model behavior

Independent observations

Comparison across scale and time

Thermal observations, ET products, field measurements, soil-water observations, and irrigation records can provide independent comparisons. Spatial scale, temporal scale, measurement uncertainty, and management conditions must be considered before interpreting differences.

Model behavior

Sensitivity, resistance, and transferability

Resistance connectivity, parameter sensitivity, stability, assumptions, and transferability remain subjects of scientific evaluation rather than being treated as fixed proof of performance everywhere.

Evidence standard: published science, software verification, computational benchmarking, and ongoing experiments remain separate categories. A result should be traceable to its test conditions and evidence source before it is used as a public claim.
