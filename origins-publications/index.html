Origins & Publications

Scientific development over time.

BAITSSS developed through research on evapotranspiration, surface energy balance, soil-water accounting, irrigation, remote sensing, and later computational and desktop-software modernization.

Origins

From satellite overpasses to a continuous modeled trajectory

The scientific problem behind BAITSSS is that important field processes continue between satellite observations. The model developed around maintaining a continuous water and energy balance through those intervals while using remote sensing, meteorological forcing, vegetation information, soil properties, and management assumptions to constrain the simulation.

Development timeline

Five stages of scientific and software development

01

Scientific foundation

Remote-sensing and surface-energy-balance research established the physical modeling foundation.

02

BAITSSS formulation

The model evolved toward a coupled two-source energy-balance and soil-water framework operating between observation times.

03

Research applications

Published studies extended evaluation across semiarid agriculture, restricted water allocation, advective conditions, lettuce, citrus, and soil-information questions.

04

Computational modernization

The scientific codebase was reworked for faster, more traceable, restartable, and reproducible execution.

05

Desktop productization

The current effort is translating the research model into a structured desktop scientific application while preserving scientific provenance and validation boundaries.

Scientific history

Research history and current software development are related, but not identical.

Historical institutions, collaborators, field sites, and research programs are part of the scientific development record of BAITSSS. That history should not be read as present sponsorship, ownership, endorsement, or responsibility for the current independent desktop-software effort unless explicitly stated.
