Development & History

Scientific history and current development.

BAITSSS has developed through work in evapotranspiration, irrigation, remote sensing, soil-water balance, field measurement, and scientific computing.

Current focus

Desktop software development, computational implementation, distribution, validation, documentation, and user workflows.

01 · Scientific history

Research and development history

The scientific development began with work at the University of Idaho, continued through research at Kansas State University, and was subsequently extended through work involving the USDA Agricultural Research Service and Desert Research Institute.

The current desktop software development builds on this research history and focuses on implementation, testing, distribution, documentation, and scientific use.

02 · Current development

Scientific model and desktop software

Current development connects the scientific model with the data preparation, project configuration, simulation, validation, results, and export workflows required for desktop use.
