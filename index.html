Scientific workflow

Prepare. Verify. Simulate. Examine. Evaluate.

BAITSSS connects the major stages of field scale modeling so the scientific path from input data to final Results remains visible and reviewable.

Prepare and verify

Define the project, spatial domain, period, environmental inputs, and model readiness before computation begins.

Simulate hourly

Carry the coupled surface energy balance and soil water state forward through the complete simulation period.

Examine and evaluate

Inspect mapped Results, pixel time series, exports, and comparisons with independent observations when available.

Scientific foundation

A process based model carried through time.

Energy balance

Soil and canopy exchange are represented separately within the surface energy balance.

Water balance

Surface and root zone stores carry water conditions between hourly steps.

Traceable workflow

Inputs, preparation, settings, assumptions, warnings, run identity, and outputs remain reviewable.

Research collaboration

Bring the scientific question. Use BAITSSS as the modeling environment.

Researchers may already have a field experiment, funded project, site, or independent dataset. BAITSSS can provide the computational framework for spatially distributed hourly evapotranspiration and soil water analysis, model evaluation, and reproducible research.
