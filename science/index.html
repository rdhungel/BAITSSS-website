Science

A continuous water and energy balance between observations.

BAITSSS combines remote sensing, meteorological forcing, vegetation state, soil-water accounting, and a two-source energy-balance framework to represent evapotranspiration through time.

Model architecture

Two-source energy balance

Soil and vegetation fluxes are represented separately and coupled through resistance formulations and the surface energy balance.

Soil-water balance

Surface and root-zone water states evolve continuously with precipitation, modeled irrigation, evaporation, transpiration, and drainage.

Observation-to-state continuity

Earth-observation inputs constrain vegetation and surface conditions while the model carries physical state forward between observation times.

Five connected scientific elements

01

Vegetation representation

Satellite-derived vegetation information is used to represent canopy development and its influence on flux partitioning.

02

Meteorological forcing

Hourly weather forcing supports reference conditions and the surface energy and water-balance calculations.

03

Resistance framework

BAITSSS uses coupled aerodynamic, canopy, and soil resistance formulations. Resistance connectivity, parameter sensitivity, stability behavior, and transferability remain active areas for systematic scientific evaluation.

04

Irrigation logic

Modeled irrigation is part of the water-balance experiment and must not be interpreted as documented farmer-applied irrigation unless independent records support that comparison.

05

Model-state feedback

Later modeled conditions depend on prior modeled states. Input quality and assumptions can therefore influence the trajectory beyond a single hour or observation.

Assumptions and scientific boundaries

BAITSSS is a physical modeling framework, not an observation of every process occurring in a field. Interpretation depends on forcing quality, vegetation representation, soil properties, rooting assumptions, irrigation information, resistance behavior, and the intended application. These boundaries are part of the scientific evaluation, not exceptions to it.
