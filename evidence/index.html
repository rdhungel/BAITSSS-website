Evidence

Separate published evidence, software verification, and ongoing scientific evaluation.

BAITSSS evidence is organized by what each source actually supports. Study-specific findings are not generalized beyond their demonstrated scope.

Published evidence

Study cards and numerical results will be transferred from the reviewed authoritative evidence table and corresponding publications. No numerical result is being recreated from memory during migration.

Software verification

Execution equivalence

Modernization tests compare new execution paths against accepted scientific reference behavior under defined equivalence criteria.

Restart behavior

Checkpoint and resume behavior is tested separately from scientific validation so lifecycle reliability can be demonstrated explicitly.

Performance

Runtime improvements are treated as engineering evidence and do not substitute for scientific validation.

Current scientific questions

Resistance behavior and sensitivity

Connectivity, parameter sensitivity, stability behavior, and transferability remain active areas of systematic evaluation.

Observation-constrained modeling

Thermal observations, independent ET products, field measurements, and irrigation records provide different forms of evidence and should be interpreted according to their scale and uncertainty.

BAITSSS · evidence categories are kept scientifically distinct.
