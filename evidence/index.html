Evidence

Scientific evidence and testing

The BAITSSS record includes peer-reviewed studies, software equivalence and reliability tests, and ongoing scientific evaluation.

Evidence categories

Published evaluations, software tests, and current experiments address different questions and are reported separately.

01 · Peer-reviewed evidence

Published evaluations

Study record

Field and site evaluations

Published BAITSSS studies report results for specified locations, periods, observations, inputs, and experimental conditions.

Publications →
Scope

Study conditions

Results apply to the crops, sites, climates, periods, inputs, observations, and comparison methods described in each publication.

02 · Software testing

Computational and software tests

Execution

Numerical equivalence

Modernized execution methods are compared with the accepted scientific reference under defined numerical criteria.

Lifecycle

Checkpoint and restart

Stop, resume, restart, and run-lifecycle behavior are tested independently of scientific evaluation.

Computation

Performance

Runtime measurements characterize computational performance and do not measure scientific accuracy.

03 · Ongoing evaluation

Current scientific evaluation

Model behavior

Resistance and sensitivity

Resistance connectivity, parameter sensitivity, stability behavior, and transferability remain subjects of evaluation.

Comparison data

Independent observations

Thermal observations, ET products, field measurements, soil-water observations, and irrigation records are compared with attention to their spatial scale, temporal scale, and uncertainty.

Published evaluations, software tests, and ongoing experiments are reported as separate evidence categories.
